Gatherings

Twenty-two sessions. Seven venues. One community.

Since August 2024 Faith Driven Singapore has met monthly to work through the eight sessions of the Faith Driven Entrepreneur Foundation Course. Here is every session, where it met and where to watch it.

How it works

A Foundations Group is an eight-session journey through the Faith Driven Entrepreneur Foundation Course. In Singapore we meet once a month on a Wednesday evening, so one cohort takes about eight months. Each evening has worship, a short FDE film about a founder, teaching, and honest table discussion. The gatherings are free. You can join at any session, and you don't need to come with a group.

What happens at a session?

  1. Arrive and eat. Food is on the table, thanks to our monthly partners.
  2. Introductions. Your life path, your path to God, what you are building and one fun fact.
  3. Worship. A song or two, often led by someone from the room.
  4. The film. A founder story and teaching from the Foundation Course.
  5. Discussion. Three or four questions from the course guide, answered honestly.
  6. Prayer. For each other's businesses, families and next steps.

The eight sessions

Each session follows one of the "marks" of a Faith Driven Entrepreneur. The full guide to the eight sessions covers them in detail.

  1. Our Call to Create: work as worship, and entrepreneurship as a calling.
  2. God Owns My Business: stewardship over ownership.
  3. Don't Worship Work: identity, burnout and Sabbath.
  4. Excellence Matters: doing good work as witness.
  5. Faithful vs. Willful: roles, priorities and surrender.
  6. Ministry in Word: talking about faith at work.
  7. Ministry in Deed: loving your neighbour through business.
  8. Global Movement: joining what God is doing worldwide.

Questions about gatherings

When does Faith Driven Singapore meet?

Foundations sessions usually meet on a Wednesday evening once a month. Check the next date and venue on Eventbrite or in the WhatsApp community.

Can I join halfway through a cohort?

Yes. Each session stands on its own, so you can join at any point and pick up the sessions you missed in the next cohort or on YouTube.

Do I need to be a Christian to attend?

The gatherings are designed for Christ-following entrepreneurs, but seekers and people of other faiths have joined us and are welcome.

Are the sessions recorded?

Most are. Recordings go on the Faith Driven Singapore YouTube channel. Two sessions of Cohort 2 were not recorded.
