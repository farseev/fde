Voices from the room

What Singapore's builders say when they talk about their calling.

Twenty-two recorded Foundations sessions, three cohorts and 776 coded sharings. Here is what founders in Singapore actually said, in their own words, with a link to each moment in the recordings.

The short version

  • Calling comes first. Calling and vocation is the most common theme among participants (187 of 550 participant sharings).
  • Discernment matters more than prosperity. Hearing God in business decisions came up 127 times. Explicit prosperity talk came up only 19 times, and pushback against it slightly outnumbered support.
  • The unicorn myth hurts. Founders spoke openly about burnout, depression and the pressure to "10x or die", and about finding an identity apart from success.
  • Faith pushes back on some Asian scripts and makes room for others. The room consistently rejected shame-of-failure and "money is success" scripts. It found gentle, character-first ways to witness among Muslim, Hindu, Buddhist and secular colleagues.
  • Community is the point. "Entrepreneurs struggle a lot… that's why the community is very important."

What themes come up most?

Every live sharing from participants was coded against a fixed list of 20 themes. The facilitator's voice is counted separately and left out here. These are the eight most common themes in participant sharings:

Calling & vocation187
Spirit guidance & prayer127
Wealth & stewardship110
Identity & success105
Ethics & excellence99
Singapore / Asian context97
Community & loneliness95
Suffering & mental health79

Prosperity & blessing appeared in just 19 sharings, the second-least common theme.

How we gathered these voices

All 22 recorded sessions on the Faith Driven Singapore YouTube channel were transcribed the same way. Each live sharing was then coded by question, theme and signal, and checked against its transcript. Curriculum films are excluded, and the facilitator's voice is counted separately from participants'. Quotes are lightly edited for readability, with filler words removed and omissions marked "…". Speakers are described by role, not by name. Transcripts come from speech recognition, so the recording is always the final word. Follow the links to hear each person say it.

Questions about this research

What do Christian entrepreneurs in Singapore talk about most?

In 550 coded participant sharings from Faith Driven Singapore sessions, the most common themes were calling and vocation (187), Spirit guidance and prayer (127), wealth and stewardship (110), and identity apart from success (105).

Do faith-driven founders in Singapore believe in the prosperity gospel?

Rarely, in these rooms. Explicit prosperity talk appeared in only 19 of 550 participant sharings, and pushback against it slightly outnumbered support. The emphasis was on discernment, meaning hearing God in business decisions, rather than on blessing.

Where can I watch the sessions?

Every recorded session is on the Faith Driven Singapore YouTube channel. Each quote on this page links straight to its moment in the video, and the gatherings page lists every session.
