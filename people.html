Where the numbers come from

Source. The Faith Driven Entrepreneur Foundations Singapore corpus: 24 sessions recorded between August 2024 and August 2026 across three cohorts, 2,349 minutes of video, 873 coded conversational turns of which 618 are participant speech from 289 distinct people. Every session was recorded and published as part of the chapter's ordinary practice.

How to read the figures. Depth is the number of doctrinal markers carried by a contribution, averaged over the contributions in a segment. Theme percentages are the share of participant contributions in that session carrying the subject, against the share across all 618 participant contributions. Facilitator share is the facilitator's proportion of turns. Subgroup contrasts use Fisher's exact test with Benjamini–Hochberg correction within families; session-level associations use Spearman rank correlation over 24 points.

Reliability. Three large language models independently recoded a stratified sample of seventy-two contributions from the codebook alone. Cohen's κ against the original coding runs 0.67, 0.63 and 0.57 averaged over the four doctrinal families, and 0.73 to 0.84 on the Pentecostal family that carries the gender finding. The models mark more sparingly than the original coding, so the proportions here are if anything conservative. The stance scheme is unattested.

Limits. The marker scheme rests on a single coding pass, and the questions were written by the convenor, so the thematic distribution reflects what was asked as well as what the room believes. Session-level parameters rest on 24 points, so the negative findings are failures to detect rather than demonstrations that no effect exists. Gender is unresolved for 100 of 420 appearance records. This is one chapter in one city and is best understood as an information-rich case rather than a sample.
